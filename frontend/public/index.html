Briefing room · Crisis negotiation rehearsal

Know what they will refuse before you ask.

Describe a negotiation you are about to walk into. DARA shows you the openings most likely to land, what the other side will push back on, and lets you hear both delegations argue it out before you say a word in the room.

Each crisis is a multi-issue bargaining problem with a private utility function per party. Agents argue it out, a judge scores how it was argued, and the settlement is measured against the best one that was actually available.

Add a crisis
  1. I
    The brief Each delegation gets its interests, red lines and private priorities. Nothing runs until you have checked the problem.
  2. II
    The debate Two delegations argue over three rounds. An independent judge scores every round, and you can correct it.
  3. III
    The advice A recommender that never read the transcript drafts your opening position for each thing you want to protect.
